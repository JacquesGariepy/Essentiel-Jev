# Product definition — Essentiel 0.2

## Promise

Give a person one coherent place to turn their own information and intentions into visible, feasible next actions, without surrendering ownership of their choices. The product should earn repeat use by solving practical problems, not by predicting every desire or increasing time spent in the interface.

“Every human will want it” is an aspiration to validate, not an implemented feature or market fact. The release offers broad optional coverage rather than forcing all life domains onto every user. A person can use tasks and shopping alone without accepting personal profiling, an AI subscription, every workflow, or a prescribed definition of success.

## Implemented value loops

| Need | Input | Computation/interaction | Inspectable result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do not lose a commitment | Original request or captured task | Local save, optional narrow classification, human review | Source-linked next action and confirmed date |
| Do not overload the day | Actual tasks, duration, available time and energy | Capacity-limited deterministic plan | Selected tasks plus visible overflow |
| Understand everyday money | Own amounts and currencies | Integer arithmetic, separate actual/estimated totals | Cashflow view, spending limit and subscription estimate |
| Prepare routine life events | Chosen domain and workflow | Edit four steps, choose dates/dependencies | Local tasks with a concrete completion result |
| Compare a choice | Explicit criteria, weights, options, constraints | Transparent manual scoring; optional separate Jev judgments | Ranked eligible options and recorded human selection |
| Reuse important information | User-written notes and references | Local organization and search | Recoverable index and follow-up tasks |
| Inspect AI instead of blindly trusting it | State, question contract, real observations | Typed validation, visible uncertainty, review packet | Reproducible request context and measured observations |
| Leave with one's own data | Workspace and export choice | Validation, optional export encryption | Portable JSON/CSV/ICS files |

## Breadth without false feature counts

The 28 domains and 112 four-step workflows cover organization, rest, money, administration, health logistics, meals, housing, safety, relationships, family, caregiving, pets, work, business, learning, creativity, enjoyment, travel, transport, community, values, environment, transitions, accessibility, possessions, handover, communication and future goals. Each domain is optional. The exact bilingual inventory is generated from the same catalog shipped in the application.

A workflow is an editable procedure that creates four ordinary local tasks. It is not a connected bank, airline booking engine, school portal, therapy system, social network or assistant that observes the user's life. The shared engines are the working functionality; the templates apply those engines to different situations.

## Personalization and accessibility

Only explicitly entered preferences are used: selected domains, language, display settings, task priorities, time/energy, costs, criteria, stated goals and notes. No inferred age, beliefs, intimate preferences, diagnoses, affiliations or interpersonal consent is required. No personalization model is trained. No one else's private mental state is inferred.

The UI includes labeled controls, keyboard-accessible native dialogs, visible focus, a skip link, local search, responsive layouts, larger text and reduced motion. These are implemented affordances, not WCAG certification or proof that all assistive technologies work. Language switching does not translate user content or make a provider equally accurate in all languages.

## Deliberate product exclusions

No addictive feed, sponsored decision ranking, streak punishment, unsolicited behavioral targeting, automatic sharing or hidden background agent. No ranking of household members or relationships. Optional profiles are shortcuts, not inferred identities. Health, legal, financial and safety templates organize information and verification steps; they do not replace qualified judgment or emergency services.

There is no login, synchronization, multiuser collaboration, connected inbox, bank feed, live travel data, push notification, media interpretation, generated reply engine, autonomous purchase/refund or hosted business platform. These are not concealed as inactive working features. Notebook document records are an index, not uploaded files.

## Evidence needed before broader claims

Adoption, time saved, reduced missed commitments, sustained usefulness, willingness to pay and model effectiveness have not been measured here. Validate one workflow with consenting users and compare an observable outcome against their current method. Keep evaluation separate from engagement optimization and from sensitive personal profiling.

Candidate observations include task/source recovery, confirmed rather than missed deadlines, time to make a plan, correction rate for Jev suggestions, duplicate-entry burden, successful backup restoration and the ability to stop using or erase the product. Collect only what a user explicitly agrees to provide. This release has no telemetry pipeline that silently gathers those measures.
