# Human needs covered — Essentiel 0.2

**28 domains, 112 activatable workflows, 448 editable steps.** Generated from the same catalog as the application. Each workflow becomes four local tasks after review and confirmation. No external service is contacted. Dates and durations are chosen, not inferred. The domains are optional and extensible, not a universal model of human desire.

## Use

Life spaces → domain → workflow → edit the steps → choose an optional first date and dependencies → confirm. A blank first date creates undated tasks. Tasks, lists, decisions and notebook remain available for needs outside the catalog. A workflow describes a human procedure, not an automatic integration with a bank, professional, organizer or platform.
